# INTERNAL DRAFT — Legal & SLA Documentation Checklist

This is a launch checklist, not legal advice and not a substitute for counsel. Do not publish commitments from this file until they are technically verified and legally approved.

## 1. Terms of Service

Define at minimum:

- who may use the service;
- account responsibilities;
- subscription/billing terms;
- usage limits and prohibited use;
- ownership/licensing of customer-supplied artwork;
- ownership/licensing of generated designs and production outputs;
- treatment of uploaded logos, photographs, brand assets, and prompts;
- responsibility for customer-provided claims/copyright/trademarks;
- revision and production-file purchase terms;
- refunds/cancellation;
- service suspension/termination;
- warranty disclaimers;
- limitation of liability;
- governing law/dispute terms;
- enterprise/order-form precedence.

## 2. Privacy Policy

Document verified facts for:

- categories of personal data collected;
- account/contact information;
- uploaded customer/vehicle/room imagery;
- prompts and project content;
- payment-provider data boundaries;
- analytics/telemetry;
- support communications;
- subprocessors/providers;
- purposes of processing;
- retention periods;
- deletion process;
- user rights by jurisdiction;
- international transfers/data residency where applicable;
- cookies/local storage;
- children's data policy;
- contact method for privacy requests.

## 3. AI / customer-content terms

Because DesignProAI accepts customer artwork, reference images, photos, and natural-language prompts, terms should address:

- user's authority to upload/use submitted content;
- use of third-party trademarks/logos supplied by the customer;
- AI-generated/recreated content limitations;
- customer responsibility for final design approval;
- difference between photorealistic proof and production geometry;
- human/automated QC scope;
- no promise that AI output alone is production-safe until the production workflow/QC completes.

## 4. Production-file terms

Clarify:

- what constitutes a production-file order;
- which revision becomes production authority;
- expected turnaround target vs. guaranteed turnaround;
- supported file formats/specifications by product;
- what QC is included;
- customer responsibility to verify printer/RIP/media-specific settings if outside DesignProAI's stated contract;
- replacement/revision policy after production output;
- storage/retention period for delivered packages;
- re-download/reorder terms.

## 5. Service Level Agreement (enterprise only unless intentionally offered broadly)

Do not write a numerical SLA until monitoring and historical reliability can support it.

Potential SLA sections:

- service definition;
- measured availability surface;
- excluded maintenance windows;
- incident severity definitions;
- response targets by severity;
- support hours/channels;
- uptime calculation method;
- service-credit calculation;
- customer responsibilities;
- force-majeure/provider exclusions;
- production-file turnaround commitment, if any;
- data recovery commitments, if any.

## 6. Support policy

Define:

- supported plans;
- support channels;
- business hours/time zone;
- emergency production-file escalation rules;
- bug vs. design-revision distinction;
- enterprise priority support;
- response targets vs. resolution targets.

## 7. Enterprise security addendum

Prepare factual answers for:

- authentication model;
- role/tenant isolation;
- production access controls;
- employee/admin access;
- logging/audit trails;
- encryption;
- backup/recovery;
- incident response;
- vulnerability management;
- subprocessors;
- deletion/termination handling.

## 8. Partner/channel agreement topics

For resellers, manufacturers, distributors, educators, and industry reps, document:

- referral vs. authorized reseller vs. strategic/OEM status;
- eligible plans/products;
- attribution window;
- recurring-revenue/commission basis;
- term and survival of recurring commissions;
- refunds/chargebacks;
- customer ownership and account control;
- brand/trademark permissions;
- sales claims partners may make;
- prohibited claims;
- support/onboarding responsibilities;
- taxes/payment timing;
- termination and treatment of existing accounts;
- enterprise deal registration/conflict rules.

## 9. Claims review before launch

Every externally published claim should be classified:

- verified in production;
- verified in staging only;
- implemented but acceptance pending;
- planned/roadmap.

High-risk claims requiring proof include:

- exact file-turnaround guarantees;
- exact PPI/file-format guarantees across every app;
- universal vehicle coverage;
- 'zero manual work' or 'zero errors';
- guaranteed print compatibility;
- uptime/reliability percentages;
- compliance/security certifications;
- fully automated physical printing/shipping.

## 10. Minimum show-ready legal set

Before taking paid enterprise/channel orders at an industry event, have at least:

1. Terms of Service;
2. Privacy Policy;
3. subscription/order terms;
4. partner/reseller agreement or signed term sheet;
5. enterprise order-form template;
6. factual security/reliability overview;
7. approved list of sales claims and current product-status disclosures.

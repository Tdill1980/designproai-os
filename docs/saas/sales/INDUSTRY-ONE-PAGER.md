# DesignProAI OS

## Prompt it. Design it. Proof it. Produce it.

**DesignProAI is the design-to-production operating system that turns a natural-language brief into professional graphics, client-ready proofs, controlled revisions, and validated production-file output — without losing the job between disconnected tools.**

A user describes what they want. **A.C.E. — AI Creative Engine — designs it.**

Then DesignProAI keeps going.

**Prompt → Design → 3D Proof → Client Review → Revision → Production → QC → Output**

## Why the industry needs it now

AI made visual ideas cheap and fast. Production is still hard.

A customer can arrive with a stunning AI concept that is the wrong size, flattened, difficult to edit, disconnected from a real vehicle, impossible to panel correctly, or completely detached from the file that eventually gets printed.

That creates a new bottleneck: **the gap between what AI can visualize and what a graphics company can manufacture.**

DesignProAI closes that gap.

## What makes it different

Think of the creative capability of professional design software, Photoshop-level image control, and the judgment of a designer with decades of real production experience — then connect that intelligence directly to the workflow that produces the file.

DesignProAI is not a single AI image generator. It is operating software.

The apps do the specialized work. The OS keeps the project connected.

## The DesignProAI ecosystem

**A.C.E. — AI Creative Engine**  
Turns a plain-language creative brief and supplied assets into professional design work.

**VehiclePro**  
Creates vehicle graphics and photorealistic proofs in the target year/make/model context.

**MyVehiclePro**  
Works from photos of the customer's actual vehicle for specialty bodies, modifications, racks, trailers, and real-world configurations.

**WallPro**  
Creates wall and environmental graphics from prompts, space photos, dimensions, and artwork.

**CutPro**  
Prepares cut and contour-cut graphics for downstream production.

**RecreatePro**  
Turns AI concepts, screenshots, old graphics, reference images, and low-resolution customer art into a DesignProAI project that can continue to production.

**RevisionStudio**  
Edit the design, manage versions, email/share client proofs, capture the approved direction, and order production-file output.

**ProductionFlow**  
Moves the approved/purchased project through the server-owned production workflow.

**GENIE Universal Panelizer**  
Resolves production geometry and panels while giving the customer a real progress page based on server state.

**GENIE Vehicle Database**  
Provides real year/make/model/configuration dimensions used by the production system instead of treating a 3D render as production geometry.

**PrintPanelStudio**  
Multi-step production QC: compare approved proof to print panel, validate geometry/identity, complete automated checks, human preflight, output verification, and final human QC.

**WrapBox**  
Delivers the verified production package.

## Why it is a real operating system

DesignProAI owns the project — not the browser tab and not a pile of exported files.

The same job carries its identity, design lineage, revisions, approval state, production order, geometry, QC state, and delivered output through the system.

The server owns the durable graph. Independent work can run when its dependencies are ready; completed work is recorded so a browser close or worker restart does not erase the job.

That means the app can change while the project stays the same.

## The production difference

Most design/AI demos stop when the picture looks good.

DesignProAI moves the approved project into manufacturing preparation:

- production geometry;
- panel relationships;
- file/output preparation;
- automated validation;
- human preflight;
- final human QC;
- verified delivery.

**The proof sells the design. The production system builds the file.**

# A.T.L.A.S. — ONE VEHICLE, ONE DESIGN AUTHORITY, ONE ARTIFACT GRAPH

**Canonical owner contract — 2026-08-31.** This document replaces the
contradictory historical shell, anonymous-field, Passenger-mirror, Call-9-panel
creation and canary instructions that previously lived here.

## 1. What A.T.L.A.S. means

A.T.L.A.S. is the exterior of one real three-dimensional target vehicle
unwrapped into one flattened two-dimensional design space, like a globe
flattened into a world atlas.

Call 1 performs one cohesive creative act across these six named surfaces:

- Driver
- Passenger
- Hood
- Roof
- Front
- Rear

They are physically related regions of one vehicle-wrap composition. They are
not six anonymous canvases, six prompts or six independent designs.

The canonical lineage is:

`OrderID → GenerationID → DesignID → RevisionID → Call-1 master → six Call-1 panels → proofs → Call 8 → Call-9 promotion → QC → ProductionPack → ZIP → WrapBox`

Technical retries retain the creative lineage and receive a new Run/AttemptID.
A customer-requested creative change retains OrderID, GenerationID and DesignID
and creates a new immutable RevisionID.

## 2. Call-1 input contract

The deployed creative endpoint is
`supabase/functions/design-panel-ai-generate/index.ts` in `atlas-artboard` mode.
The droplet runtime maps verified inputs to that endpoint; it does not assemble
a second creative prompt and does not make another Call-1 Gemini request.

Call 1 receives:

- authoritative Commercial or ReStyle mode;
- the customer's unmodified creative placement language and structured brief;
- business name, exact supplied contact text, industry, palette, typography,
  mascot and verified customer references when present;
- canonical year, make and model;
- server-resolved body class;
- named Passenger/Driver/Rear/Roof/Hood/Front region mapping;
- one deterministic, unlabeled and unstroked six-region guide;
- the proven DesignPanelAI vehicle-wrap designer persona and quality floor.

The prompt explicitly teaches that the result is one target vehicle exterior
unfolded into one cohesive wrap. Driver and Passenger are coordinated
adaptations of the same design system, but each is its own Call-1 region.
Neither may be independently redesigned or substituted with the other's bytes.

GENIE/code remains authority for numerical geometry, dimensions, masks, crop
coordinates and lineage. Those values do not become printable prompt furniture.
The model-facing guide contains no labels, dimensions, strokes, silhouette,
header or footer that can be copied into the artwork. The separate human guide
may display technical annotations.

### Pickup coverage

For a pickup:

- exterior cab and bed sides receive the coordinated side artwork;
- the tailgate exterior receives Rear artwork;
- the bed floor and inner bed walls remain unwrapped;
- deterministic placement/projection masks enforce those exclusions;
- every canonical source panel remains an opaque, uninterrupted, full-bleed
  rectangle with artwork beneath later masks.

Wheel wells, glass, lights, trim and open-bed interiors never punch holes into
canonical Call-1 source artwork.

### Reference status

No structural 3D ↔ flattened example pair is active in production today. A
flat-only Flamingo image is not a matching pair and must not be silently
attached. If later evidence proves a teaching example is necessary, use at most
one owner-approved, hash-pinned, genuinely matching wrapped-vehicle ↔ flattened
A.T.L.A.S. pair. It teaches only the mapping relationship and contributes no
customer artwork, wording, logo, palette, style or production authority.

The distressed Martini Porsche used a customer-requested one-off camera angle.
Neither that angle nor an aspect-ratio change is the design-quality diagnosis.

## 3. Call-1 acceptance gates

Call 1 may be blocked only when the artifact is structurally invalid or unsafe
for the deterministic graph, including:

- missing/invalid canonical master bytes;
- missing or invalid required region/container coverage;
- invalid opacity/required source geometry;
- invalid dimensions or contract shape;
- invalid content hash, storage identity or lineage;
- deterministic evidence that the output is a diagram/checkerboard rather than
  valid source artwork.

Semantic/visual AI review is advisory. It may report quality, wording,
coherence or brief-fidelity concerns, but it may not refuse or delay a
deterministically valid Call-1 authority merely because the design is
aesthetically imperfect.

Pixel similarity between Passenger and a mirrored Driver is not a structural
gate. The runtime must not rewrite Passenger by copying/mirroring Driver.

## 4. Call-1 outputs and immediate publication

Call 1 accepts and durably binds together:

- one immutable A.T.L.A.S. master;
- one immutable RevisionID/revisionSequence;
- six canonical Call-1 panel files separated deterministically from the six
  authored regions;
- master and panel hashes, dimensions, surface mapping, prompt/model/contract
  versions, timing and provenance.

`cutCallOnePanels` separates and stores the already-authored regions. It is not
a creative extraction stage and does not reconstruct artwork.

As soon as persistence succeeds:

| Surface | Immediate state |
|---|---|
| RevisionStudioIQ | accepted design, GenerationID, DesignID, RevisionID, prompt/mode/vehicle, revision history, master and six Call-1 panels |
| PanelProStudio | the same identity, master, six Call-1 panels, dimensions, hashes, mapping and provenance |

Neither UI waits for 3D views, Call 8, Call 9, Full QC or ProductionPack to
discover the job or display the canonical Call-1 authority. Both UIs consume
server state and never create substitute identities or artwork.

## 5. Presentation proofs

Each required proof is a presentation descendant of its matching Call-1
surface panel:

| View | Artwork authority | Camera authority |
|---|---|---|
| Driver | Driver Call-1 panel | locked Driver angle |
| Passenger | Passenger Call-1 panel | locked Passenger angle; nose points right |
| Hood | Hood Call-1 panel | locked Hood angle |
| Front | Front Call-1 panel plus declared adjacent context | locked Front angle |
| Rear | Rear Call-1 panel plus declared adjacent context | locked Rear angle |
| Roof | Roof Call-1 panel | locked top-down Roof angle |
| Close-Up | explicitly selected Call-1 detail surface | locked Close-Up angle |

`persona-photographer-render` owns presentation, camera, studio, lighting and
physical vinyl realism. It may not redesign, recompose, recolor, substitute or
become production artwork authority.

Passenger must be rendered from the persisted Passenger panel. It must not be
a mirrored Driver proof or Driver artwork used as a fake Passenger.

Independent descendants may run concurrently after their declared panel
authority exists. Driver may receive scheduling priority for customer latency,
but it is not a prerequisite or artwork authority for Passenger or other views.

## 6. Call 8 and Call 9

Call 8 builds the production proof from the six existing Call-1 panels. It may
present and prove them; it may not create, crop, extract or modify artwork.

Call 9 is deterministic production promotion:

1. re-read the six accepted Call-1 panel objects;
2. verify bytes, hashes, surface identity, master binding, GenerationID,
   DesignID and RevisionID;
3. reject drift or substitution;
4. add production-promotion receipts/status for those unchanged bytes.

Call 9 is not an image-generation call, is not a panel creator and is not the
first PanelProStudio visibility boundary.

## 7. PanelProStudio authority and safety

The recent-jobs index is identity-only. Selecting a row must hydrate the full
server job by GenerationID before the workspace renders. A thin row must never
be cast to a full job.

Call-1 panels are immediately visible and clearly labeled as canonical/view
only. Human production QC, corrections and enhancement remain restricted to a
Call-9-promoted production artifact. A browser approval, selected revision,
draft checklist or preflight checkbox may never carry across a GenerationID or
RevisionID boundary.

The board reads promoted panels from
`job.concept_json.qc_side_panels`; there is no top-level
`job.qc_side_panels` contract.

## 10. Failure and retry rule

On failure, trace the exact graph stage and its direct dependencies. Apply the
smallest correct fix, deploy the exact release, and resume/retry the same
lineage where the contract permits.

A technical failure never mints a new GenerationID or creative revision. A new
RevisionID is created only when the customer's creative request changes or the
owner explicitly requests a new creative version. A completed verified stage
receipt is reused and never recomputed unless an existing invalidation contract
or explicit new revision requires it.
